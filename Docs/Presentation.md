Welcome to the Cooperative Mobility Network

Imagine you belong to a local taxi cooperative. You want to offer your customers seamless rides when they travel to a neighboring city. In return, you agree to serve their citizens when they visit your city. This is a great partnership, known as a reciprocity agreement.

But there is a catch. It is called the Trust Problem.

How do two independent cooperatives trust that the exchange will be fair? If we provide dozens of rides to their customers, will they actually provide rides to ours? If the exchange is uneven, one side ends up doing all the work for free. In the past, fixing this required expensive contracts, upfront deposits, or just blind faith.

We solve the trust problem using a powerful idea from Game Theory.

Instead of relying on blind trust, we use a shared digital ledger. This ledger tracks every token spent and watches the actual flow of passengers moving between the two cooperative territories.

To keep everything fair, the system uses a dynamic token conversion rate. Think of it like a pair of scales that automatically balances itself.

Let's say Cooperative A is providing a lot of rides to visitors from Cooperative B. However, Cooperative B is providing very few rides in return. The scale is now unbalanced.

When this happens, the system automatically adjusts the exchange rate based on the estimated flow of traffic. The tokens from the cooperative doing more of the work become more valuable. This shift naturally encourages the system to rebalance. It acts just like the famous "Tit for Tat" strategy in game theory: it starts by cooperating, and then mirrors the behavior of the other side to ensure fairness.

It is a completely self-correcting system. It rewards fair play and mathematically protects both cooperatives from being taken advantage of.

You do not need to worry about complex negotiations or unequal partnerships. The algorithm handles the trust for you, ensuring that every token spent is a fair trade.
